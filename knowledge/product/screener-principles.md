# Screener principles

## Product positioning

Use only:

- **Platform-informed screening simulation**
- **Evidence-based application readiness assessment**

Never claim an exact ATS score, guaranteed pass, employer-specific rank or reproduction of Greenhouse, Lever, Workday or SAP private logic.

## Core principles

1. Evaluate observable, job-related evidence only.
2. Exclude name, age/date of birth, gender/pronouns, photograph, ethnicity/race, religion, disability/health, marital/family status, political affiliation and other protected/sensitive attributes from scoring.
3. Do not use location as a proxy for ethnicity or socioeconomic background. Use work authorization/location only for explicit, lawful role constraints.
4. Never infer personality, health, family status, demographics or “culture fit.”
5. Never fabricate an ATS score or private employer configuration.
6. Separate: parseability, baseline eligibility, qualification alignment, evidence strength, recruiter readability and interview readiness.
7. Cite resume and job-description evidence for every material finding.
8. Represent missing evidence separately from confirmed lack; preserve unknown.
9. Explain every score/recommendation in plain language and expose uncertainty.
10. Suggest truthful improvements; prohibit fabrication and keyword stuffing.
11. Preserve model/rubric/version, inputs, evidence links, transformations, output and reviewer action.
12. Require meaningful human review for adverse or high-impact action.
13. Test accessibility, subgroup performance, calibration and adverse impact before deployment and after material change.
14. Minimize personal data, enforce purpose limitation and support correction/deletion/appeal workflows.

## Scenario model

- **Scenario A — Manual review:** parsing and structured presentation support a recruiter.
- **Scenario B — Eligibility rules:** explicit answers are evaluated against stated mandatory criteria.
- **Scenario C — Optional matching:** an explained role-specific match supports prioritization.
- **Scenario D — Third-party screening:** a named provider performs a separately documented assessment.

Results must show scenario assumptions and never silently select one.

## Observable organisational competencies

If role-relevant, use evidence for cross-functional collaboration, written communication, stakeholder management, decision-making, customer discovery or delivery leadership. Never combine these into “culture fit.”
