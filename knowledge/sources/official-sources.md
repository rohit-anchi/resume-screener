# Official sources

## Selection policy

- Tier 1 operational help, administrator guides, learning content and release material are the factual baseline.
- Official marketing pages support availability/positioning only; implementation details require help documentation.
- Dates, entitlements and release scope are versioned. Revalidate before implementation.

## Priority collections

- **Greenhouse:** GH-01–GH-12. Highest-value controls are application rules, Application Review, candidate filters, scorecards and Real Talent/Talent Matching.
- **Lever:** LV-01–LV-12. Keep profile/opportunity, ordinary search, Talent Fit, VONQ screening and interview feedback as distinct mechanisms.
- **Workday:** WD-01–WD-10. Treat the Job Application business process as tenant-configured; keep native parsing, configured ranking, Candidate Skills Match and HiredScore separate.
- **SAP SuccessFactors:** SAP-01–SAP-09. Separate classic prescreen scoring/auto-disqualification from licensed AI-assisted skills matching.
